Subject: Announcement of SandwichClip Discontinuation
From: Amelia Grant <amelia.grant@precisionpaperclip.com> 
To: All Employees <all.employees@precisionpaperclip.com>
Date: May 05, 2017, at 9:00 AM

Dear Team,

I want to take a moment to communicate directly with all of you regarding the recent decision to discontinue the SandwichClip product line. After careful consideration and evaluation of our Q1 financial results, it has become clear that continuing to invest in this product is neither prudent nor sustainable given the losses we have experienced.

This decision was not made lightly, and I want to acknowledge and thank each of you who contributed your time and efforts to the SandwichClip project. Your dedication has not gone unnoticed, and your hard work is appreciated.

As we shift our focus back to our core paperclip products, I encourage everyone to remain engaged and positive during this transition. Our commitment to quality remains strong, and together, I believe we can build on our successes in the future.

If anyone has questions or would like to discuss this further, please feel free to reach out to your managers or come speak to me directly.

Thank you for your understanding and support.

Best,  
Amelia
