Hi Team,

Before heading into our design sprint meeting, I wanted to share a quick summary of key insights from the user testing feedback that we should address:

1. **Usability**: Users appreciated the ergonomic design but felt the grip could be improved for comfort.
2. **Performance**: Many testers highlighted the importance of durability, especially the tension strength during repeated use.
3. **Aesthetics**: Color options generated a mixed response; some preferred a more classic look while others leaned towards modern colors.

Let’s brainstorm how we can address these observations during our design discussions!
