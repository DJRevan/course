As we think about enhancing our promotional strategy for the SandwichClip™, I've been brainstorming a few ideas that could help boost visibility and drive sales.

2. **Collaborative Promotions:** Partnering with sandwich shops or delis could create bundled offers that introduce the SandwichClip™ into their sales mix, thus increasing exposure in an organic setting.

3. **Social Media Campaigns:** With the buzz around food and health trends, we might consider a campaign showcasing the practicality of our clips in meal prep and packing lunches, combined with user-generated content incentives.
