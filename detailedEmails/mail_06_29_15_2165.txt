I wanted to share a preliminary analysis of the employee sentiment survey results that closed over the weekend. Overall, participation was robust, with approximately 78% of employees responding.

The key findings include:

1. **Overall Sentiment**: 70% of employees feel satisfied with the current safety measures, although a considerable portion expressed concerns about the clarity of our communication regarding upcoming changes.
2. **Specific Concerns**: Many responses highlighted a fear of the unknown, particularly around the timeline for implementing new safety protocols.
3. **Suggestions**: Employees have suggested more frequent updates and clearer timelines moving forward.

I believe it's crucial that we address these findings in a follow-up communication and consider scheduling a meeting to discuss how we can enhance our transparency around safety measures and timelines.

Let me know your thoughts on how we should proceed.
