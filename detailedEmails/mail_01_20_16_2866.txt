I wanted to share some of the insights gathered from the initial buyer feedback since the launch of the SandwichClip™. It seems category managers at the three pre-sold chains are pleased with the product, and they appreciate the shelf-ready packs we provided. However, I've also encountered concerns from some independent retailers who continue to question the practicality of using a paperclip for sandwiches.

To address their concerns, I think we should consider scheduling follow-up demos or increasing point-of-purchase materials that illustrate real-life applications. I’m confident that with further engagement, we can convert skeptics into advocates.
