Hi Team,

As promised, I have compiled the initial findings from the distributor interviews I conducted yesterday. Here are some key points:

- Many buyers referred to the SandwichClip™ as more of a novelty item rather than a practical tool for everyday use.
- Several expressed concerns over the pricing compared to traditional clips, suggesting it might not be justified for their customers.
- A few mentioned that in-store demonstrations could potentially shift perceptions and encourage trial.

I believe we need to rethink our marketing messaging to highlight the practicality of the SandwichClip™ in daily uses. I’ll be working on a more detailed report to share shortly.
