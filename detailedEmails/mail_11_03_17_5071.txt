I wanted to share the notes from our QA framework meeting yesterday regarding the EP-1 pilot testing. Here are the key points we discussed:

* Accelerated aging tests will be conducted at 40°C/75% RH.
* Shelf-life checks will happen at 3-, 6-, and 12-month intervals.
* Stress tolerance metrics include clamp force, bend tests, and repeated use cycles.
* We confirmed the dissolution timing must remain under 30 seconds in room-temperature water.

Please ensure your teams are aligned with these protocols as we move forward in the testing phase.
