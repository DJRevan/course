**Dax Randall Shepard** (born January 2, 1975)[2] is an American actor, comedian, and filmmaker. Since 2018, he has hosted _[Armchair Expert](/wiki/Armchair_Expert "Armchair Expert")_ , a [podcast](/wiki/Podcast "Podcast") in which he interviews celebrities, journalists, and academics about their lives. 

Shepard has appeared in the feature films _[Without a Paddle](/wiki/Without_a_Paddle "Without a Paddle")_ (2004), _[Zathura: A Space Adventure](/wiki/Zathura:_A_Space_Adventure "Zathura: A Space Adventure")_ (2005), _[Employee of the Month](/wiki/Employee_of_the_Month_\(2006_film\) "Employee of the Month \(2006 film\)")_ (2006), _[Idiocracy](/wiki/Idiocracy "Idiocracy")_ (2006), _[Let's Go to Prison](/wiki/Let%27s_Go_to_Prison "Let's Go to Prison")_ (2006), _[Hit and Run](/wiki/Hit_and_Run_\(2012_film\) "Hit and Run \(2012 film\)")_ (2012), and _[CHiPs](/wiki/CHiPs_\(film\) "CHiPs \(film\)")_ (2017), the last two of which he also wrote and directed. Shepard portrayed [Crosby Braverman](/wiki/List_of_Parenthood_characters#Crosby_Braverman "List of Parenthood characters") in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC "NBC") comedy-drama series _[Parenthood](/wiki/Parenthood_\(2010_TV_series\) "Parenthood \(2010 TV series\)")_ from 2010 to 2015. He played Luke Matthews in the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") show _[The Ranch](/wiki/The_Ranch "The Ranch")_ , co-starred in [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company")'s _[Bless This Mess](/wiki/Bless_This_Mess_\(TV_series\) "Bless This Mess \(TV series\)")_ and acted in the [MTV](/wiki/MTV "MTV") practical joke reality series _[Punk'd](/wiki/Punk%27d "Punk'd")_ (2003). 

He is a co-founder of the diaper and baby product company Hello Bello with his wife [Kristen Bell](/wiki/Kristen_Bell "Kristen Bell"). 

## Early life, family and education

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Dax_Shepard&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life, family and education")]

Shepard was born at Beyer Hospital in [Ypsilanti, Michigan](/wiki/Ypsilanti,_Michigan "Ypsilanti, Michigan"), a city in southeastern [Michigan](/wiki/Michigan "Michigan").[3][4] He is the son of Laura Louise LaBo,[5][6] who worked at [General Motors](/wiki/General_Motors "General Motors") (GM), and David Robert "Dave" Shepard Sr.,[7][8][9] who was a car salesman.[8][10] He is of part French-Canadian descent.[1] His parents divorced when he was 3 years old.[3] Shepard was sexually abused at age 7, which he believes was a major underlying cause of his later substance abuse issues. He did not reveal the abuse to anyone until 12 years after it occurred.[11]

Shepard's mother had developed a substantial business by the time he was in high school.[5] She worked car shows with traveling events at racetracks. She started as a janitor on the midnight shift at GM,[12] then worked in fleet management at the GM proving grounds in [Milford, Michigan](/wiki/Milford,_Michigan "Milford, Michigan"), then hosted hospitality days for all the GM family members, eventually moving into public relations at an ad agency.[10] She returned to GM, becoming the owner of four shops that managed publicity events for magazine journalists. From 14 to 18, Shepard worked for his mother on the road, going from racetrack to racetrack.[3] For about two years, Shepard had a stepfather who was an engineer on the [Corvette](/wiki/Chevrolet_Corvette "Chevrolet Corvette").[3][10] Shepard's mother has been married four times; Shepard said he has had three stepfathers.[13]

According to Shepard, his mother named him for the rich playboy Dax (Diogenes Alejandro Xenos), in [Harold Robbins](/wiki/Harold_Robbins "Harold Robbins")' novel _The Adventurers_.[14] He has an older brother, David Shepard Jr., who lives in Oregon,[8] and a younger half-sister, Carly Hatter,[3] whom he cast in two films: 2012's _[Hit and Run](/wiki/Hit_and_Run_\(2012_film\) "Hit and Run \(2012 film\)")_[15][16] and 2017's _[CHiPs](/wiki/CHiPs_\(film\) "CHiPs \(film\)")_. Through marriage, he is distantly related to [George Washington](/wiki/George_Washington "George Washington") on his mother's side.[17]

Shepard has said that he was raised in Milford,[3] although he lived in many suburbs of Detroit, growing up primarily in [Walled Lake, Michigan](/wiki/Walled_Lake,_Michigan "Walled Lake, Michigan"). Shepard is [dyslexic](/wiki/Dyslexia "Dyslexia") and said it inspired his acting career.[18] He graduated from [Walled Lake Central High School](/wiki/Walled_Lake_Central_High_School "Walled Lake Central High School") in 1993[19] before enrolling in [The Groundlings school](/wiki/The_Groundlings "The Groundlings").[20]

After attending [Santa Monica College](/wiki/Santa_Monica_College "Santa Monica College") and [West Los Angeles College](/wiki/West_Los_Angeles_College "West Los Angeles College"), Shepard transferred to [UCLA](/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles "University of California, Los Angeles"), where he graduated _[magna cum laude](/wiki/Magna_cum_laude "Magna cum laude")_ with a B.A. in anthropology.[21] He had a deal with his mother that if he went to college she would pay his rent.[3]

## Career

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Dax_Shepard&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")]

[](/wiki/File:Dax_Shepard_at_the_2008_Tribeca_Film_Festival.JPG)Shepard at the 2008 [Tribeca Film Festival](/wiki/Tribeca_Film_Festival "Tribeca Film Festival") in New York City

After a year in [Santa Barbara, California](/wiki/Santa_Barbara,_California "Santa Barbara, California"), he moved to Los Angeles in 1996.[3] He found out about [The Groundlings](/wiki/The_Groundlings "The Groundlings") improv troupe from Santa Barbara friend, Kareem Elseify, who had spent time there. Shepard ended up auditioning (the first time he acted) and wound up taking improvisation and sketch comedy classes there, while attending UCLA. After about five years of classes, he got into the Sunday Company of The Groundlings—in a group that included [Melissa McCarthy](/wiki/Melissa_McCarthy "Melissa McCarthy"), [Octavia Spencer](/wiki/Octavia_Spencer "Octavia Spencer"), [Fortune Feimster](/wiki/Fortune_Feimster "Fortune Feimster"), [Tate Taylor](/wiki/Tate_Taylor_\(filmmaker\) "Tate Taylor \(filmmaker\)"), and [Nat Faxon](/wiki/Nat_Faxon "Nat Faxon").[3]

From 2003 onwards, Shepard appeared in the improv _[Candid Camera](/wiki/Candid_Camera "Candid Camera")_ -type show _[Punk'd](/wiki/Punk%27d "Punk'd")_ , with [Ashton Kutcher](/wiki/Ashton_Kutcher "Ashton Kutcher"). When the show had its 2012 revival, he continued to appear. Shepard said he auditioned for _Punk'd_ and, while doing the pilot, became friendly with Kutcher, who arranged for him to get an agent.[3] He had auditioned unsuccessfully for ten years before landing the role in _Punk'd_ , his first.[3]

In 2004, Shepard starred in the comedy _[Without a Paddle](/wiki/Without_a_Paddle "Without a Paddle")_ , alongside [Seth Green](/wiki/Seth_Green "Seth Green") and [Matthew Lillard](/wiki/Matthew_Lillard "Matthew Lillard"). Despite negative reviews, the film was a commercial success that, as of 2009, grossed more than US$65 million worldwide.[22] In 2005, he starred as The Astronaut in _[Zathura: A Space Adventure](/wiki/Zathura:_A_Space_Adventure "Zathura: A Space Adventure")_ , a science fiction adventure fantasy film. In 2006, he appeared with [Dane Cook](/wiki/Dane_Cook "Dane Cook") and [Jessica Simpson](/wiki/Jessica_Simpson "Jessica Simpson") in the comedy _[Employee of the Month](/wiki/Employee_of_the_Month_\(2006_film\) "Employee of the Month \(2006 film\)")_ and in Mike Judge's film _[Idiocracy](/wiki/Idiocracy "Idiocracy")_ as the character Frito. 

During the same time, Shepard began appearing in more films and landed his first main character role in _[Let's Go to Prison](/wiki/Let%27s_Go_to_Prison "Let's Go to Prison")_ (2006), alongside [Will Arnett](/wiki/Will_Arnett "Will Arnett") and [Chi McBride](/wiki/Chi_McBride "Chi McBride"). He had a main role in the 2008 comedy _[Baby Mama](/wiki/Baby_Mama_\(film\) "Baby Mama \(film\)")_ , starring opposite [Tina Fey](/wiki/Tina_Fey "Tina Fey") and [Amy Poehler](/wiki/Amy_Poehler "Amy Poehler"). Shepard wrote the script for the [Paramount](/wiki/Paramount_Pictures "Paramount Pictures") venture _Get 'Em Wet_ , where he again appeared with Arnett. In 2010, he wrote, directed, and starred in the low-budget satirical [mockumentary](/wiki/Mockumentary "Mockumentary") feature _[Brother's Justice](/wiki/Brother%27s_Justice "Brother's Justice")_ ; the film won an Audience Award at the 2010 [Austin Film Festival](/wiki/Austin_Film_Festival "Austin Film Festival"). He had a supporting role in the 2010 [romantic comedy](/wiki/Romantic_comedy "Romantic comedy") film _[When in Rome](/wiki/When_in_Rome_\(2010_film\) "When in Rome \(2010 film\)")_ , which starred his future wife [Kristen Bell](/wiki/Kristen_Bell "Kristen Bell"). 

From 2010 to 2015, Shepard was part of the main cast of the NBC drama _[Parenthood](/wiki/Parenthood_\(2010_TV_series\) "Parenthood \(2010 TV series\)")_ , playing Crosby Braverman.[23][24][25]

[](/wiki/File:Kristen_Bell_Dax_Shepard.jpg)[Kristen Bell](/wiki/Kristen_Bell "Kristen Bell") and Dax Shepard

Shepard wrote, produced, co-directed, and starred in the 2012 low-budget film _[Hit and Run](/wiki/Hit_and_Run_\(2012_film\) "Hit and Run \(2012 film\)")_ alongside Bell and his close friend [Bradley Cooper](/wiki/Bradley_Cooper "Bradley Cooper").[26][27] He said the movie, which includes numerous car chases and fast driving scenes, allowed him to live out his _[Smokey and the Bandit](/wiki/Smokey_and_the_Bandit "Smokey and the Bandit")_ fantasy.[3]

Shepard had a supporting role in the 2014 film _[The Judge](/wiki/The_Judge_\(2014_film\) "The Judge \(2014 film\)")_.[28]

In September 2014, Shepard starred with his wife, [Kristen Bell](/wiki/Kristen_Bell "Kristen Bell"), in a commercial for the Samsung Galaxy Tab S.[29] It was so popular (with over 20 million YouTube views) that they did another commercial for the holiday season.[30]

Shepard wrote and directed _[CHiPs](/wiki/CHiPs_\(film\) "CHiPs \(film\)")_ , based on the 1977 to 1983 American [crime drama](/wiki/Crime_film "Crime film") _[CHiPs](/wiki/CHiPs "CHiPs")_ , where he starred as Officer Jon Baker alongside [Michael Peña](/wiki/Michael_Pe%C3%B1a "Michael Peña") as Frank "Ponch" Poncherello.[28] The project was released by Warner Bros.[31][32][33] on March 24, 2017, to negative reviews. 

On February 14, 2018, Shepard launched the [podcast](/wiki/Podcast "Podcast") _[Armchair Expert](/wiki/Armchair_Expert "Armchair Expert")_ with co-host [Monica Padman](/wiki/Monica_Padman "Monica Padman"). The show explores the stories of their guests. Featured guests on the show have included [Barack Obama](/wiki/Barack_Obama "Barack Obama"), [Bill Gates](/wiki/Bill_Gates "Bill Gates"), [Kristen Bell](/wiki/Kristen_Bell "Kristen Bell"), [Ashton Kutcher](/wiki/Ashton_Kutcher "Ashton Kutcher"), [Will Ferrell](/wiki/Will_Ferrell "Will Ferrell"), [Julia Louis-Dreyfus](/wiki/Julia_Louis-Dreyfus "Julia Louis-Dreyfus"), and hundreds of others. On June 7, 2018, the show released their first episode of "Experts on Expert", in which Shepard and Padman interview experts in their fields. Featured experts on the show have included psychologist [Wendy Mogel](/wiki/Wendy_Mogel "Wendy Mogel"), comedian and author [David Sedaris](/wiki/David_Sedaris "David Sedaris"), and speechwriter [Jon Favreau](/wiki/Jon_Favreau_\(speechwriter\) "Jon Favreau \(speechwriter\)"). The show was the most popular new podcast on [iTunes](/wiki/ITunes "ITunes") in 2018.[34] On December 3, 2019, it was revealed that Shepard will be a host of the revived _[Top Gear America](/wiki/Top_Gear_America "Top Gear America")_ , which premiered on [Motor Trend](/wiki/Motor_Trend "Motor Trend") in January 2021.[35] In 2024, the company secured a first-look deal with Wondery, a unit of [Amazon](/wiki/Amazon_\(company\) "Amazon \(company\)"), after the success of the podcast show _Armchair Expert_.[36] In 2024, Armchair Expert was the 8th biggest podcast in the USA on [Spotify's](/wiki/Spotify "Spotify") platform.[37]

## Personal life

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Dax_Shepard&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Personal life")]

### Relationships

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Dax_Shepard&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Relationships")]

[](/wiki/File:Kristen_Bell,_Dax_Shepard_2012.jpg)Shepard and [Kristen Bell](/wiki/Kristen_Bell "Kristen Bell") at the 2012 [DoSomething](/wiki/DoSomething "DoSomething") Awards

Shepard met actress [Kristen Bell](/wiki/Kristen_Bell "Kristen Bell"), also a native of Detroit's northern suburbs, at the birthday party of a mutual friend; they began dating in late 2007. The couple announced their engagement in January 2010; they decided to delay marriage until the state of California passed legislation legalizing [same-sex marriage](/wiki/Same-sex_marriage "Same-sex marriage").[38][39] After section 3 of the [Defense of Marriage Act](/wiki/Defense_of_Marriage_Act "Defense of Marriage Act") was ruled unconstitutional by the [Supreme Court](/wiki/U.S._Supreme_Court "U.S. Supreme Court") on June 26, 2013, Bell asked Shepard through Twitter to marry her,[40] which he accepted.[41] They were married at the Beverly Hills County Clerk's Office on October 17, 2013.[42] They have two daughters, born in March 2013[43] and December 2014.[44]

### Substance use problem

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Dax_Shepard&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Substance use problem")]

Shepard has said that, except for a year in high school when he used drugs, he did not have a [substance abuse](/wiki/Substance_abuse "Substance abuse") problem until he was 18 years old.[3] After struggling for years with alcohol, cocaine, and pills,[45] Shepard achieved sobriety in September 2004.[3] On September 25, 2020, in a special episode of his podcast, _Armchair Expert_ , Shepard revealed he had relapsed while recovering from an accident by using painkillers to augment a prescription. He announced that he was again sober, with seven days of sobriety as of the recording date (September 21, 2020).[46][47]

### Interests and charity work

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Dax_Shepard&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Interests and charity work")]

Shepard races motorcycles at [Buttonwillow Raceway Park](/wiki/Buttonwillow_Raceway_Park "Buttonwillow Raceway Park"), including his [Ducati Hypermotard](/wiki/Ducati_Hypermotard "Ducati Hypermotard") 1100S and [Suzuki GSX-R1000](/wiki/Suzuki_GSX-R1000 "Suzuki GSX-R1000").[48] He donates much of his time to the Hollenbeck Youth Center, an after-school program that provides opportunity to at-risk inner-city youth. He has served as their official Master of Ceremonies, along with his friend [Tom Arnold](/wiki/Tom_Arnold_\(actor\) "Tom Arnold \(actor\)"), for the [Inner-City Games](/wiki/Inner-City_Games "Inner-City Games") and Hollenbeck Youth Center's Miracle on 1st Street Toy Giveaway Program in East Los Angeles.[49][50]

He is an avid car enthusiast.[51] He owns the 1967 [Lincoln Continental](/wiki/Lincoln_Continental "Lincoln Continental") that was featured in the movie _[Hit and Run](/wiki/Hit_and_Run_\(2012_film\) "Hit and Run \(2012 film\)")_ , and races off-road.[3]

Shepard and his wife were advocates of California Senate Bill 606, called "no-kids paparazzi".[52] Bell said: "We're not saying that we can't be newsworthy. We're saying that our child is not newsworthy."[53] California Senate Bill 606[54] passed in 2013, with testimony by actresses [Halle Berry](/wiki/Halle_Berry "Halle Berry") and [Jennifer Garner](/wiki/Jennifer_Garner "Jennifer Garner").[52]

Shepard practices [Transcendental Meditation](/wiki/Transcendental_Meditation "Transcendental Meditation").[55]
