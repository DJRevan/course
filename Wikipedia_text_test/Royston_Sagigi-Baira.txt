**Royston Sagigi-Baira** , also known as **Royston Noell** ,[1] is an Australian singer best known for winning the [eighth season](/wiki/Australian_Idol_\(season_8\) "Australian Idol \(season 8\)") of _[Australian Idol](/wiki/Australian_Idol "Australian Idol")_ in 2023. He officially won the title in March 2023, $100,000 prize money and a recording contract with [Sony Music Australia](/wiki/Sony_Music_Australia "Sony Music Australia").[2]

## Early life

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Royston_Sagigi-Baira&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")]

Royston Sagigi-Baira is a Thanakwith (Aboriginal) and Wagadagam (Torres Strait Islander) man from [Mapoon, Queensland](/wiki/Mapoon,_Queensland "Mapoon, Queensland").[3]

Sagigi-Baira reflected saying "Most kids wanted to play rugby or go fishing, but I just wanted to sing It's all I ever wanted to do.".[4] In year 2, his teacher gave him _[High School Musical](/wiki/High_School_Musical "High School Musical")_ DVD as a prize, saying "I took it home and watched it over and over again and completely fell in love with music, especially singing."[4]

Sagigi-Baira sang in church and in school. Sagigi-Baira attended the [Aboriginal Centre for the Performing Arts](/wiki/Aboriginal_Centre_for_the_Performing_Arts "Aboriginal Centre for the Performing Arts") (ACPA) in Brisbane and as of 2023, is in his second year of a Bachelor of Fine Arts (Music) degree at the [Queensland University of Technology](/wiki/Queensland_University_of_Technology "Queensland University of Technology").[4]

## Career

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Royston_Sagigi-Baira&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")]

### 2018–2022: _All Together Now_

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Royston_Sagigi-Baira&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: 2018–2022: All Together Now")]

In 2018, Sagigi-Baira competed on [the Australian version](/wiki/All_Together_Now_\(2018_Australian_TV_series\) "All Together Now \(2018 Australian TV series\)") of _[All Together Now](/wiki/All_Together_Now_\(franchise\) "All Together Now \(franchise\)")_ , becoming the first contestant to snag a perfect score of 100 performiong [Sam Smith](/wiki/Sam_Smith "Sam Smith")'s "[Lay Me Down](/wiki/Lay_Me_Down_\(Sam_Smith_song\) "Lay Me Down \(Sam Smith song\)")"[5] eventually been the last contestant eliminated behind Lai Utovou and Tarryn Stokes.[6]

During [NAIDOC Week](/wiki/NAIDOC_Week "NAIDOC Week") in July 2022, Aboriginal Christian leader, poet and writer [Brooke Prentis](/wiki/Brooke_Prentis "Brooke Prentis") spoke with Sagigi-Baira on national radio about the theme "Get Up! Stand up! Show up!"[7]

In November 2022, Sagigi-Baira released his debut single "When I Fall Apart" under the name Royston Noell.[8][9]

### 2023: _Australian Idol_

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Royston_Sagigi-Baira&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: 2023: Australian Idol")]

Main article: [Australian Idol (season 8)](/wiki/Australian_Idol_\(season_8\) "Australian Idol \(season 8\)")

In 2023, Sagigi-Baira audition for the [Seven Network](/wiki/Seven_Network "Seven Network") reboot of _[Australian Idol](/wiki/Australian_Idol "Australian Idol")_ with the song "[I'm Not the Only One](/wiki/I%27m_Not_the_Only_One "I'm Not the Only One")" by [Sam Smith](/wiki/Sam_Smith "Sam Smith"), receiving a golden ticket to the top 50.[10][11] He went on to advance through from the top 50 to the top 24, and then made it to the top 12 live shows.[12][13]

Sagigi-Baira advanced through to the top ten, then top eight and then top 6 before finding himself in the grand finale, and final three.[14][15]

Upon being announced as a grand finalist Noell said "I can't believe this. This is crazy. This is a testament [to the fact] that no matter where you come from, as long as you work hard, you can follow your dreams, and they can come true."[16]

After a performance on Whitney Houston's "When You Believe", Sagigi-Baira was announced as the winner.[17][18][19]

Immediately after the announcement, his winners single "Invincible", written by [Tones and I](/wiki/Tones_and_I "Tones and I"), was released.[20][16] The song was compared to [Rihanna](/wiki/Rihanna "Rihanna")'s "[Diamonds](/wiki/Diamonds_\(Rihanna_song\) "Diamonds \(Rihanna song\)")" on Radio [6PR](/wiki/6PR "6PR").[21]

In November 2023, he released "Dreaming".[22]

In November 2024, Noell released "Feeling Good" and announced the release of his debut EP _Sunrise_.[23]

## Personal life

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Royston_Sagigi-Baira&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Personal life")]

Sagigi-Baira is currently residing in Cape York, in far north Queensland.[24]
