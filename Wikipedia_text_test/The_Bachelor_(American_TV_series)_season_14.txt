_The Bachelor: On the Wings of Love_ is the **fourteenth season** of ABC reality television series _[The Bachelor](/wiki/The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\) "The Bachelor \(American TV series\)")_. The season premiere aired on January 4, 2010. The show features 31-year-old [Jake Pavelka](/wiki/Jake_Pavelka "Jake Pavelka"), a pilot from [Dallas](/wiki/Dallas "Dallas"), [Texas](/wiki/Texas "Texas"), courting 25 women. 

Pavelka finished in seventh place on [season 5](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_\(American_TV_series\)_season_5 "The Bachelorette \(American TV series\) season 5") of _[The Bachelorette](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_\(American_TV_series\) "The Bachelorette \(American TV series\)")_ featuring [Jillian Harris](/wiki/Jillian_Harris "Jillian Harris"). It is the first season of _The Bachelor_ to be broadcast in [high definition](/wiki/High-definition_television "High-definition television"). The season concluded on March 1, 2010, with Pavelka choosing to propose to 23-year-old marketing rep Vienna Girardi. The couple ended their engagement in June 2010.

### Future appearances

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Future appearances")]

#### _Dancing with the Stars_

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Dancing with the Stars")]

Jake Pavelka competed in the [tenth season](/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_\(American_season_10\) "Dancing with the Stars \(American season 10\)") of _[Dancing with the Stars](/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_\(American_TV_series\) "Dancing with the Stars \(American TV series\)")_. He partnered with [Chelsie Hightower](/wiki/Chelsie_Hightower "Chelsie Hightower") and finished in 7th place. 

#### _The Bachelorette_

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: The Bachelorette")]

[Ali Fedotowsky](/wiki/Ali_Fedotowsky "Ali Fedotowsky") was chosen as the bachelorette for the [sixth season](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_\(season_6\) "The Bachelorette \(season 6\)") of _[The Bachelorette](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_\(American_TV_series\) "The Bachelorette \(American TV series\)")_. 

#### _Bachelor Pad_

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Bachelor Pad")]

Runner-up [Tenley Molzahn](/wiki/Tenley_Molzahn "Tenley Molzahn"), along with contestants [Gia Allemand](/wiki/Gia_Allemand "Gia Allemand"), Ashley Elmore, Elizabeth Kitt, Michelle Kujawa and Jessie Sulidis, returned for the inaugural season of _[Bachelor Pad](/wiki/Bachelor_Pad#Season_1_\(2010\) "Bachelor Pad")_. Michelle was eliminated in week 1, Jessie in week 2, Gia in week 3, and Ashley in week 5. Elizabeth and her partner, Jesse Kovacs, were eliminated at the beginning of week 6, finishing in 3rd place. Tenley and her partner, Kiptyn Locke, were eliminated at the end of week 6, finishing as the runners-up. 

Jake, Gia, Vienna Girardi, and Ella Nolan, returned for the second season of _Bachelor Pad_. Gia quit in week 2, and Jake was eliminated in week 3. Ella and her partner, Kirk DeWindt, were eliminated at the beginning of week 6, finishing in 4th place. Vienna and her partner, Kasey Kahl, were eliminated at the end of week 6, finishing in 3rd place. 

#### _Bachelor in Paradise_

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Bachelor in Paradise")]

**[Season 1](/wiki/Bachelor_in_Paradise_\(American_season_1\) "Bachelor in Paradise \(American season 1\)")**

Michelle returned for the inaugural season of _[Bachelor in Paradise](/wiki/Bachelor_in_Paradise_\(American_TV_series\) "Bachelor in Paradise \(American TV series\)")_. She quit in week 1. 

**[Season 2](/wiki/Bachelor_in_Paradise_\(American_season_2\) "Bachelor in Paradise \(American season 2\)")**

Tenley returned for the second season of _Paradise_. She broke up with Joshua Albers in week 6. 

#### Other appearances

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Other appearances")]

Outside of the _Bachelor Nation_ franchise, Tenley and Michelle appeared as contestants in the _Bachelors vs. Bachelorettes_ special on the [season 7](/wiki/Wipeout_\(season_7\) "Wipeout \(season 7\)") of _[Wipeout](/wiki/Wipeout_\(2008_game_show\) "Wipeout \(2008 game show\)")_.

### After the Final Rose

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: After the Final Rose")]

This is the special episode that airs after every finale. In season 14's segment, Jake told Chris Harrison about _Bachelor Pad_ , Ali was chosen as the next bachelorette in the [sixth season of _The Bachelorette_](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_\(season_6\) "The Bachelorette \(season 6\)"). ### Deaths

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Deaths")]

#### Gia Allemand

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Gia Allemand")]

On August 14, 2013, third-place contestant Gia Allemand died after a reported suicide attempt. Several contestants and Pavelka expressed their condolences via Twitter, with Pavelka saying, "I am in complete shock and devastated to hear the news about Gia. She was one of the sweetest people I have ever known. And a very dear friend. My heart goes out to her family during this very difficult time. We have lost an angel today. I miss you Gia...".[19][20][21]

#### Alexa McAllister

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Alexa McAllister")]

On 16 February 2016, Alexa (Lex) McAllister died after an apparent suicide attempt in [Columbus, Ohio](/wiki/Columbus,_Ohio "Columbus, Ohio"), as reported by TMZ. Per E! News, the local police said that on 13 February, they'd received a call in which it was claimed that McAllister had overdosed on prescription drugs. While McAllister was in a stable condition on her way to [Grant Medical Center](/wiki/Grant_Medical_Center "Grant Medical Center"), E! News got access to a police report, which stated that she had taken "a lot" of pills. Her family took her off life support after her health began to deteriorate. McAllister was 31.[22][23] Pavelka tweeted his condolences on Twitter, "I'm so sad to hear about Alexa. Such a beautiful girl. My heart breaks for her family. Covering them in prayer during this rough time".[24]

## Jake Pavelka

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Bachelor_\(American_TV_series\)_season_14&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Jake Pavelka")]

Main article: [Jake Pavelka](/wiki/Jake_Pavelka "Jake Pavelka")

Jake Pavelka grew up in [Denton, Texas](/wiki/Denton,_Texas "Denton, Texas"), and attended [University of North Texas](/wiki/University_of_North_Texas "University of North Texas") and [Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University](/wiki/Embry-Riddle_Aeronautical_University "Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University"). In his free time, he takes dancing lessons, woodworking, and has fun flying acrobatic planes and he is also a former child actor. Pavelka is an airline pilot, and took his first flying lessons at 12 years old. He also became a certified flight instructor when he was 23 years old.[25]

Pavelka became a member of an ABC reality show for the third time when he appeared as a celebrity contestant on the [tenth season](/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_\(American_TV_series\)_season_10 "Dancing with the Stars \(American TV series\) season 10") of _[Dancing with the Stars](/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_\(American_TV_series\) "Dancing with the Stars \(American TV series\)")_. Partnered with [Chelsie Hightower](/wiki/Chelsie_Hightower "Chelsie Hightower"), he was the fifth competitor eliminated on April 27, 2010.
