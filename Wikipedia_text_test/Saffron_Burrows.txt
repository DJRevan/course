**Saffron Burrows** (born 22 October 1972)[1] is a British actress who has appeared in films such as _[Circle of Friends](/wiki/Circle_of_Friends_\(1995_film\) "Circle of Friends \(1995 film\)"),_ _[Wing Commander](/wiki/Wing_Commander_\(film\) "Wing Commander \(film\)"),_ _[Deep Blue Sea](/wiki/Deep_Blue_Sea_\(1999_film\) "Deep Blue Sea \(1999 film\)"),_ _[Gangster No. 1](/wiki/Gangster_No._1 "Gangster No. 1"),_ _[Enigma](/wiki/Enigma_\(2001_film\) "Enigma \(2001 film\)"),_ _[Troy](/wiki/Troy_\(film\) "Troy \(film\)"),_ _[Reign Over Me](/wiki/Reign_Over_Me "Reign Over Me")_ , and _[The Bank Job](/wiki/The_Bank_Job "The Bank Job")_. On the small screen she starred as [Lorraine Weller](/wiki/Lorraine_Weller "Lorraine Weller") on _[Boston Legal](/wiki/Boston_Legal "Boston Legal"),_ Dr. Norah Skinner on _[My Own Worst Enemy](/wiki/My_Own_Worst_Enemy_\(TV_series\) "My Own Worst Enemy \(TV series\)"),_ [Detective Serena Stevens](/wiki/Detective_Serena_Stevens "Detective Serena Stevens") on _[Law& Order: Criminal Intent](/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Criminal_Intent "Law & Order: Criminal Intent"),_ and [Victoria Hand](/wiki/Victoria_Hand_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\) "Victoria Hand \(Marvel Cinematic Universe\)") on _[Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/wiki/Agents_of_S.H.I.E.L.D. "Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.")_. She appeared as Cynthia Taylor on the [Prime Video](/wiki/Prime_Video "Prime Video") series _[Mozart in the Jungle](/wiki/Mozart_in_the_Jungle "Mozart in the Jungle")_ and as [Dottie Quinn](/wiki/Dottie_Quinn "Dottie Quinn") in the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") series _[You](/wiki/You_\(TV_series\) "You \(TV series\)")_. 

## Early life

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Saffron_Burrows&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")]

Saffron Burrows was born in [St Pancras](/wiki/St_Pancras,_London "St Pancras, London") in central [London](/wiki/London "London") and grew up in [Stoke Newington](/wiki/Stoke_Newington "Stoke Newington"). Her mother is a trade unionist and primary school teacher in [Hackney](/wiki/London_Borough_of_Hackney "London Borough of Hackney") and her father is an architect and teacher. Her parents and stepfather were [Socialist Workers Party](/wiki/Socialist_Workers_Party_\(UK\) "Socialist Workers Party \(UK\)") members, and Burrows was politically active from a young age.[2][3]

Burrows attended William Tyndale Primary School in [Islington](/wiki/Islington "Islington") and then [Stoke Newington School](/wiki/Stoke_Newington_School "Stoke Newington School") in [Stoke Newington](/wiki/Stoke_Newington "Stoke Newington"). When asked for her preference of a school in Hackney or [Hampstead](/wiki/Hampstead "Hampstead"), she said she wanted to be in a multicultural and inclusive environment. She enrolled in acting classes at the [Anna Scher Theatre](/wiki/Anna_Scher_Theatre "Anna Scher Theatre") in Islington when she was 11.[4][5]

Burrows had a successful modelling career after she was discovered at age 15 in [Covent Garden](/wiki/Covent_Garden "Covent Garden") by fashion photographer Beth Boldt.[6][7] For five years she divided her time between London and [Paris](/wiki/Paris "Paris"), where she learned [French](/wiki/French_language "French language").[8] She found the modelling world's "obsession with the ideal of the body" troubling.[6][3]

## Career

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Saffron_Burrows&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")]

### Acting

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Saffron_Burrows&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Acting")]

Burrows made her film debut in 1993 with a small role in [Jim Sheridan](/wiki/Jim_Sheridan "Jim Sheridan")'s _[In the Name of the Father](/wiki/In_the_Name_of_the_Father "In the Name of the Father")_. Her first significant acting roles came in 1995, as an ambitious young Irishwoman in _[Circle of Friends](/wiki/Circle_of_Friends_\(1995_film\) "Circle of Friends \(1995 film\)"),_ and in [Ngozi Onwurah](/wiki/Ngozi_Onwurah "Ngozi Onwurah")'s _Welcome II The Terrordome_.[9] In 1996 she starred in the [BBC](/wiki/BBC "BBC") production of [Dennis Potter](/wiki/Dennis_Potter "Dennis Potter")'s _[Karaoke](/wiki/Karaoke_\(TV_series\) "Karaoke \(TV series\)")_[10] and in _[Hotel de Love](/wiki/Hotel_de_Love "Hotel de Love")_ ; the next year she was seen in _[Lovelife](/wiki/Lovelife "Lovelife")_ , _[Nevada](/wiki/Nevada_\(1997_film\) "Nevada \(1997 film\)")_ , _[The Matchmaker](/wiki/The_Matchmaker_\(1997_film\) "The Matchmaker \(1997 film\)")_ and [Mike Figgis](/wiki/Mike_Figgis "Mike Figgis")'s _[One Night Stand](/wiki/One_Night_Stand_\(1997_film\) "One Night Stand \(1997 film\)")_.[11] In 1999 she appeared in Figgis's experimental film _[The Loss of Sexual Innocence](/wiki/The_Loss_of_Sexual_Innocence "The Loss of Sexual Innocence")_ , in which she played twins – one raised in England, the other in Italy.[12] In 1999, she appeared in the thriller _[Deep Blue Sea](/wiki/Deep_Blue_Sea_\(1999_film\) "Deep Blue Sea \(1999 film\)"),_ and had the title role in Figgis's film adaptation _[Miss Julie](/wiki/Miss_Julie_\(1999_film\) "Miss Julie \(1999 film\)")_ , which premiered at the [Toronto International Film Festival](/wiki/Toronto_International_Film_Festival "Toronto International Film Festival").[13] She starred for Figgis again alongside [Stellan Skarsgård](/wiki/Stellan_Skarsg%C3%A5rd "Stellan Skarsgård") in _[Timecode](/wiki/Timecode_\(film\) "Timecode \(film\)")_ (2000), a split-screen digital experimental film shot in a single take with no edits. She followed it with _[Gangster No. 1](/wiki/Gangster_No._1 "Gangster No. 1")_ , starring [Malcolm McDowell](/wiki/Malcolm_McDowell "Malcolm McDowell"), [Paul Bettany](/wiki/Paul_Bettany "Paul Bettany") and [David Thewlis](/wiki/David_Thewlis "David Thewlis").[11] She next co-starred with [Kate Winslet](/wiki/Kate_Winslet "Kate Winslet") and [Dougray Scott](/wiki/Dougray_Scott "Dougray Scott") in [Michael Apted](/wiki/Michael_Apted "Michael Apted")'s 1940s drama _[Enigma](/wiki/Enigma_\(2001_film\) "Enigma \(2001 film\)")_[14][15] and starred in _[Tempted](/wiki/Tempted_\(film\) "Tempted \(film\)")_ , an improvised thriller set in New Orleans, with [Burt Reynolds](/wiki/Burt_Reynolds "Burt Reynolds") and [Peter Facinelli](/wiki/Peter_Facinelli "Peter Facinelli").[16]

Figgis's ensemble feature _[Hotel](/wiki/Hotel_\(2001_film\) "Hotel \(2001 film\)")_ followed, reuniting Burrows with colleagues from _Timecode_ including [Salma Hayek](/wiki/Salma_Hayek "Salma Hayek") and [Danny Huston](/wiki/Danny_Huston "Danny Huston") on location in Venice, where she played the Duchess of Malfi. She had a cameo in _[Frida](/wiki/Frida_\(2002_film\) "Frida \(2002 film\)")_ , Hayek's 2002 biopic of artist [Frida Kahlo](/wiki/Frida_Kahlo "Frida Kahlo"). Burrows performed in Spanish in _The Galindez File_ , a film written by Spanish novelist [Vazquez Montalban](/wiki/Vazquez_Montalban "Vazquez Montalban"), about a woman seeking the truth about the disappearance of a critic of the [Dominican](/wiki/Dominican_Republic "Dominican Republic") dictator Trujillo.[17] In 2004, she played the role of [Andromache](/wiki/Andromache "Andromache"), the wife of Trojan Prince [Hector](/wiki/Hector "Hector"), played by [Eric Bana](/wiki/Eric_Bana "Eric Bana"), in _[Troy](/wiki/Troy_\(film\) "Troy \(film\)")_. 

Burrows dedicated herself to stage work in the early 2000s. She appeared at the [Royal National Theatre](/wiki/Royal_National_Theatre "Royal National Theatre") in [Jeanette Winterson](/wiki/Jeanette_Winterson "Jeanette Winterson")'s _The Powerbook_ , directed by [Deborah Warner](/wiki/Deborah_Warner "Deborah Warner"); the play also went on tour, visiting the Theatre National Du Chaillot in Paris and the Teatro Argentina in Rome. In January 2005, she created the role of Janey Morris in the world premiere of _Earthly Paradise_ at the [Almeida Theatre](/wiki/Almeida_Theatre "Almeida Theatre").[18] The play centered on the love triangle of Janey, her husband [William Morris](/wiki/William_Morris "William Morris") (the writer and proponent of the [Arts and Crafts](/wiki/Arts_and_Crafts "Arts and Crafts") movement), and the [Pre-Raphaelite](/wiki/Pre-Raphaelite "Pre-Raphaelite") painter [Dante Gabriel Rossetti](/wiki/Dante_Gabriel_Rossetti "Dante Gabriel Rossetti").[19] London theatre critic Nicholas De Jongh said of her performance: "Burrows takes to the stage like a swan to water ... She deserves no end of watching."[20] On 30 October 2005, she appeared on stage at the [Old Vic](/wiki/Old_Vic "Old Vic") theatre in London in a rehearsed reading of the 24-hour play _Night Sky_ , alongside [Christopher Eccleston](/wiki/Christopher_Eccleston "Christopher Eccleston").[21][22] Burrow has since participated in the "24 Hour Plays" in New York and Los Angeles.[23]

In 2006, Burrows was the female lead in the New Zealand thriller _[Perfect Creature](/wiki/Perfect_Creature "Perfect Creature")_ , appeared in [Hal Hartley](/wiki/Hal_Hartley "Hal Hartley")'s film _[Fay Grim](/wiki/Fay_Grim "Fay Grim")_ , and co-starred in _[Klimt](/wiki/Klimt_\(film\) "Klimt \(film\)")_ , Chilean director [Raoul Ruiz](/wiki/Raoul_Ruiz "Raoul Ruiz")' cinematic portrait of [Gustav Klimt](/wiki/Gustav_Klimt "Gustav Klimt"). Burrows played opposite [John Malkovich](/wiki/John_Malkovich "John Malkovich") as the artist's lover, a woman of many personalities and nationalities.[11] Onstage in 2006, she starred opposite [David Schwimmer](/wiki/David_Schwimmer "David Schwimmer") in the world premiere of [Neil LaBute](/wiki/Neil_LaBute "Neil LaBute")'s _[Some Girl(s)](/wiki/Some_Girl\(s\) "Some Girl\(s\)")_ at the Gielgud Theatre, London.[24] She then appeared in [Mike Binder](/wiki/Mike_Binder "Mike Binder")'s film _[Reign Over Me](/wiki/Reign_Over_Me "Reign Over Me")_ with [Don Cheadle](/wiki/Don_Cheadle "Don Cheadle") and [Adam Sandler](/wiki/Adam_Sandler "Adam Sandler"). Burrows also had lead roles in the Indian thriller _Broken Thread_ and in director [Peter Howitt](/wiki/Peter_Howitt_\(actor\) "Peter Howitt \(actor\)")'s drama _Dangerous Parking_.[11] On television, she played attorney [Lorraine Weller](/wiki/Lorraine_Weller "Lorraine Weller") on [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company")'s _[Boston Legal](/wiki/Boston_Legal "Boston Legal")_ (season 4) from 2007 to 2008. She starred in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC "NBC") series _[My Own Worst Enemy](/wiki/My_Own_Worst_Enemy_\(TV_series\) "My Own Worst Enemy \(TV series\)")_ in 2008. 

Also in 2008, Burrows starred in the independent film _[The Guitar](/wiki/The_Guitar_\(film\) "The Guitar \(film\)")_ as a woman who suffers several life setbacks all at once, including a cancer diagnosis and bankruptcy, and survives them all. [Amy Redford](/wiki/Amy_Redford "Amy Redford")'s directorial debut, the film premiered at the [Sundance Film Festival](/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival").[25] She also had a starring role in Roger Donaldson's heist film _[The Bank Job](/wiki/The_Bank_Job "The Bank Job")_ ,[26] and appeared opposite [Kevin Spacey](/wiki/Kevin_Spacey "Kevin Spacey") in Jonas Pate's _[Shrink](/wiki/Shrink_\(film\) "Shrink \(film\)")_. She contributed to an _Actors Come Clean for Congo_ video for the [Enough Project](/wiki/Enough_Project "Enough Project")'s campaign in support of [conflict mineral](/wiki/Resource_war "Resource war") issues.[27] In 2010, she starred as [Detective Serena Stevens](/wiki/Detective_Serena_Stevens "Detective Serena Stevens") on _[Law& Order: Criminal Intent](/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Criminal_Intent "Law & Order: Criminal Intent")_,[28] departing at the end of the ninth season. In September 2010, she took part in the documentary feature _The People Speak_ , televised on the [History Channel](/wiki/History_Channel "History Channel"). The film was directed and produced by [Colin Firth](/wiki/Colin_Firth "Colin Firth") and Anthony Arnove. Burrows modelled for [Marks & Spencer](/wiki/Marks_%26_Spencer "Marks & Spencer")'s autumn 2010 campaign for their [Portfolio](/wiki/List_of_Marks_%26_Spencer_brands#Portfolio "List of Marks & Spencer brands") range.[29] In 2012, she performed opposite [Rob Lowe](/wiki/Rob_Lowe "Rob Lowe") in the political comedy _Knife Fight_.[11]

In 2013 and 2014, Burrows joined the [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company") television show _[Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/wiki/Agents_of_S.H.I.E.L.D. "Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.")_ , part of the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe"). She played agent [Victoria Hand](/wiki/Victoria_Hand_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\) "Victoria Hand \(Marvel Cinematic Universe\)") in a recurring role.[30] She also starred in the [Amazon Video](/wiki/Amazon_Video "Amazon Video") series _[Mozart in the Jungle](/wiki/Mozart_in_the_Jungle "Mozart in the Jungle")_ as Cynthia Taylor, a cellist with the New York Symphony. The series ran for four seasons from 2014 to 2018.[31] In 2019, Burrows starred in the recurring role of [Dottie Quinn](/wiki/Dottie_Quinn "Dottie Quinn") on the [second season](/wiki/You_\(season_2\) "You \(season 2\)") of the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") thriller _[You](/wiki/You_\(TV_series\) "You \(TV series\)")_.[32] She reprised her role in the [third season](/wiki/You_\(season_3\) "You \(season 3\)"), which was released in October 2021.[33]

### Writing

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Saffron_Burrows&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Writing")]

Burrows has written diaries, book reviews and newspaper and magazine articles for _[The Guardian](/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")_ , _[The Independent](/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent")_ , and _[The Times](/wiki/The_Times "The Times")_ and the _[New Statesman](/wiki/New_Statesman "New Statesman")_.[34][35]

## Personal life

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Saffron_Burrows&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Personal life")]

Burrows is a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Arts](/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Arts "Royal Society of Arts").[11] She is [bisexual](/wiki/Bisexual "Bisexual"), and said that she "prefers the company of women". She was engaged to actor [Alan Cumming](/wiki/Alan_Cumming "Alan Cumming") in the 1990s and dated director [Mike Figgis](/wiki/Mike_Figgis "Mike Figgis") for five years until 2002.[36] She was previously in a relationship with actress [Fiona Shaw](/wiki/Fiona_Shaw "Fiona Shaw"). Burrows married writer Alison Balian, her girlfriend of six years, in August 2013. Burrows gave birth to their first child in 2012 and their daughter in 2017.[37][38] Burrows and Balian separated in 2020.[39]

Burrows has expressed sympathy for European-style social democracies and for French [Socialist politician](/wiki/Socialist_Party_\(France\) "Socialist Party \(France\)") [Ségolène Royal](/wiki/S%C3%A9gol%C3%A8ne_Royal "Ségolène Royal").[40] She joined an [anti-racism](/wiki/Anti-racism "Anti-racism") group when she was 11 years old and went on to become the Vice President of the National Civil Rights Movement.[41] Burrows is a campaigner for disabled rights and equality.[42] In 2009, she became an American citizen.[43]
